## Character design
- Light-skinned Black man with a 1970s look, light freckles
- Round reddish-brown afro
- Matching reddish-brown horseshoe-style mustache running down both sides of the mouth, not connecting under the chin; no sideburns, sides of the face clean-shaven
- Thin-framed aviator-style glasses with light brown tinted lenses
- Exaggerated, quirky face with a long, clearly elongated neck
- Powder blue bell-bottom suit (signature outfit; the saved reference image shows a camel coat and cream shirt instead, so ask for the suit in prompts)
