## Character design (locked Oct 29, 2026)
- Light-skinned Black man with a 1970s look, freckles, hazel eyes
- Large round reddish-brown afro
- Light brown tinted aviator glasses
- Reddish-brown U-shaped horseshoe mustache, ends down both sides of the mouth, not connected under the chin; no sideburns
- Signature trait: long, slightly exaggerated neck (the master close-up's proportion is the standard; full-body generations tend to shorten it, so check every new image). J rejected an over-stretched profile neck as too long and distorted, so keep it subtle in full-body views.
- Outfit: bright orange shawl-collar dinner jacket, sky blue ruffled tuxedo shirt, black velvet bow tie, light blue pocket square, dark green rose-print bell-bottoms, black dress shoes (replaced the earlier powder blue suit idea)

## Lessons learned
- Nano Banana 2 edits tend to raise the collar and shorten the neck. Compositing the original head and neck over the edited clothing holds the proportion reliably. For full-body views, a small local lengthening spread over the whole neck looks natural; a big stretch looks distorted.
